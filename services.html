Our Story

Founded in 2018, Artisan Crust Bakery started with a simple wood-fired oven and a passion for slow-fermented sourdough. Over the years, we have grown into a beloved community staple.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide our local community with wholesome, handcrafted baked goods produced through sustainable practices.

Vision: To become the leading sustainable artisan bakery known for exceptional quality and heritage recipes.
